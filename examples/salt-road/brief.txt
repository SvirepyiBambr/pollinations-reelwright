Title: The Salt Road.

A 50-second mini-documentary explaining why the sea is salty. Follow one drop of rainwater from a granite mountain to the ocean. Five beats, in order:

1. Rain lands on a granite mountain and begins to run downhill.
2. A mountain stream cuts through rock and carries dissolved minerals.
3. The river widens and carries its mineral load toward the sea.
4. The sun heats the ocean; water rises as vapour, salt stays behind.
5. Salt crystals on the shore at dusk; the sea keeps what the sky returns.

Visual style: editorial gouache illustration, muted teal and amber palette, textured cold-press paper, soft grain, cinematic wide 16:9 composition, natural light.
